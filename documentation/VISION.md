# 1. Vision

FrontDesk aims to become the digital operating layer for small and local businesses.

The long-term vision is to give every business a simple digital system through which it can:

- establish its digital presence,
- manage its business information,
- communicate with customers,
- receive enquiries and orders,
- understand business activity,
- automate repetitive work,
- use AI to make better decisions,
- and eventually operate through AI agents.

The platform should make sophisticated digital capabilities accessible to business owners who do not have technical knowledge.

---

# 2. The Core Idea

Most small businesses already have the information required to exist digitally.

Their information may be scattered across:

- paper menus,
- PDF menus,
- Excel files,
- WhatsApp,
- Instagram,
- Google Business,
- existing websites,
- photographs,
- price boards,
- business cards,
- and the owner's knowledge.

The problem is not necessarily the absence of information.

The problem is that the information is:

- fragmented,
- difficult to maintain,
- difficult to publish,
- difficult to connect,
- and difficult to use for automation.

FrontDesk aims to turn this fragmented information into a structured digital business.

---

# 3. Initial Product Promise

The initial product promise is:

> **Import your business → Make it digital → Run it on WhatsApp.**

A business owner should be able to provide the business information they already have.

FrontDesk should then help transform it into:

- a digital business profile,
- a mobile-first website,
- a catalog/menu,
- a QR entry point,
- and a customer enquiry channel.

The owner should not need to understand:

- web development,
- databases,
- hosting,
- APIs,
- HTML,
- CSS,
- JavaScript,
- SEO,
- or AI technology.

# 4. The Problem We Are Solving

## 4.1 Technical dependency

Many small businesses depend on developers or agencies for seemingly simple website changes.

For example:

> "Add this product."

> "Change this price."

> "Remove this item."

> "Change our opening hours."

> "Add this festival offer."

FrontDesk aims to allow the business owner to perform these changes themselves.

7. Business Knowledge Base

The Business Knowledge Base is a fundamental concept in FrontDesk.

It represents the structured understanding of a business.

At the initial stage it may contain:

8. AI Philosophy

FrontDesk should use AI to reduce complexity for the business owner.

AI should not exist merely because the product is an AI product.

AI should perform useful work such as:

understanding imported business information,
extracting products,
generating descriptions,
organizing information,
answering questions using business data,
proposing website changes,
helping the owner update the business.

The guiding principle is:

The owner describes what they want. FrontDesk handles the technical complexity.

For example:

"Add chicken shawarma for ₹150."

or:

"Make the homepage look more premium."

The owner should not need to manually manipulate code.

9. AI Safety Philosophy

AI should not silently make important changes.

FrontDesk should eventually use:

User Request
      ↓
AI Understanding
      ↓
Proposed Changes
      ↓
Preview
      ↓
Owner Approval
      ↓
Apply

Important business information must remain under the owner's control.

AI should not invent:

prices,
opening hours,
availability,
addresses,
contact information,
or business policies.

When information is unknown, the system should acknowledge that it does not know.

The first version is intentionally small.

FrontDesk v0.1 should prove the following hypothesis:

A non-technical business owner will provide their existing business information, use FrontDesk to turn it into a digital presence, publish it, create a QR entry point, and use the platform to receive customer enquiries.

v0.1 therefore focuses on:

11. What FrontDesk Is Not

FrontDesk is not initially intended to be:

a generic AI coding tool,
a direct competitor to v0-style code generation,
a full Webflow replacement,

Some of these capabilities may eventually be integrated or supported, but they are not the starting point.

15. Product Principles
Principle 1 — Business first

The business is the primary object, not the website.

Principle 2 — Simple for owners

Technical complexity should remain behind the interface.

Principle 3 — One source of truth

Business information should be structured and reusable.

Principle 4 — AI must be useful

AI should solve work, not merely generate text.

Principle 5 — Owner remains in control

Important AI actions require appropriate approval.

Principle 6 — Mobile first for customers

Customers should be able to access a business without installing an application.

Principle 7 — Build for small businesses

The product should not feel like enterprise software.

Principle 8 — Progressive complexity

Do not expose advanced capabilities until they become useful.

Principle 9 — Measure real business value

A published website is not enough.

Customer interaction and repeat usage matter more.

Principle 10 — Build the foundation before the ecosystem

The Business Knowledge Base must be reliable before advanced automation and AI agents are introduced.

16. Success Definition

FrontDesk v0.1 is successful if real businesses can independently complete:

Import business
      ↓
Review information
      ↓
Publish
      ↓
Create QR
      ↓
Customer visits
      ↓
Customer contacts business

The primary validation question is:

Do business owners continue using FrontDesk after their website is created?

The most important long-term validation metric is therefore 30-day retained businesses, not the number of websites generated.

17. Vision Statement

FrontDesk exists to give every small business a simple digital front door and, eventually, an intelligent operating system.

### Why this is our first document

`VISION.md` deliberately **doesn't define exact features, APIs, database tables, technology choices, or screen layouts**. Those belong in the later documents.

It gives every future AI/developer a stable answer to:

> **"What are we actually trying to build, and why?"**

Once we approve this vision, **Document #2 should be `BRD.md` — the Business Requirements Document**, where we turn this vision into the actual business problem, market assumptions, stakeholders, business objectives, KPIs, constraints, and success criteria.
